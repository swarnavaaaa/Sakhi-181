# Welcome to Our New Blog

We are excited to launch our new blog to share stories, resources, and updates about gender-based violence support. Our mission at Sakhi 181 is to provide verified, location-based access to one-stop centers.

## What to Expect

In this space, we will be sharing:

- **Impact Stories**: Real stories of resilience and empowerment.
- **Resource Guides**: Helpful information on accessing medical, legal, and emotional support.
- **Updates**: News about our network of centers across Telangana.

We believe that by sharing knowledge and stories, we can empower more women to seek the help they deserve.

### Getting Help

Remember, if you or someone you know is facing gender-based violence, you are not alone. You can always call the verified government helpline at **181** for immediate emergency assistance.

*Together, we can create a safer community for everyone.*
