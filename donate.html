Frequently Asked Questions

Is my donation tax-exempt?
Yes, all donations made to Sakhi 181 Foundation are eligible for tax exemption under section 80G of the Income Tax Act. We will email you the tax receipt within 7-10 working days of your contribution.
How will my donation be used?
Your contribution directly funds:
  • Build and maintain an easy-to-use, secure website for quick access to resources.
  • Conduct ongoing field verification to keep listings accurate and reliable.
Can I donate regularly?
Absolutely! You can set up a standing instruction with your bank to automatically transfer a specific amount every month to our account. Monthly donations provide us with sustainable support to plan long-term rehabilitation programs.
