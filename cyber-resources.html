Additional Cyber Safety Resources

Directory of emergency helplines, official reporting portals, automated takedown platforms, cyber hygiene handbooks, and pro-bono legal/mental health support.

Back to Cyber GBV Hub Module 2 of 2

Comprehensive Cyber Safety & Awareness Directory: Compiled from the Lok Sabha Committee Report on Cyber Crimes Against Women, Ministry of Home Affairs (I4C), MeitY, NCW, and verified public safety portals.

1. Reporting & Emergency Helplines

Immediate emergency contacts and government portals when a cybercrime or harassment incident occurs.

National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (NCRP)

Official 24x7 Government of India portal to report any cybercrime. Features a confidential/anonymous reporting option specifically for "Women & Child Related Cyber Crime".

cybercrime.gov.in

Cybercrime Helpline — 1930

Ministry of Home Affairs 24x7 toll-free helpline for financial cyber fraud and immediate cyber crime reporting. Agents alert financial intermediaries in real-time to freeze funds.

Call 1930 Helpline

NCW National Women Helpline — 14490

24x7 dedicated helpline operated by the National Commission for Women (NCW) for women facing violence or online harassment; links callers to police, hospitals, and One Stop Centres.

Call 14490 Helpline

Women Helpline — 181 (Sakhi OSC)

Ministry of Women & Child Development 24x7 helpline offering integrated police facilitation, medical aid, legal counsel, and temporary shelter for women in crisis.

Call 181 Helpline

NALSA Free Legal Aid Helpline — 15100

National Legal Services Authority helpline connecting eligible women (especially those unable to afford counsel) to pro-bono panel advocates for cybercrime representation.

Call 15100 NALSA

Report & Check Suspect Feature

Government of India feature on cybercrime.gov.in enabling citizens to search known cybercriminal identifiers (phone numbers, emails, URLs, accounts) and report suspicious ones.

Check Suspect Identifiers

2. Legal Rights, Laws & Grievance Redressal

Official appellate bodies, statutory rights, and legal escalation mechanisms under IT Rules 2021 & BNS 2023.

Grievance Appellate Committee (GAC)

Government appeal mechanism under IT Rules 2021 for cases where a tech platform's Grievance Officer fails to remove unlawful content (NCII, deepfakes, impersonation) or respond within time.

Visit GAC.gov.in

NCW "Her Legal Guide" App & Web

NCW legal handbook in plain English and Hindi explaining women's statutory rights, cyber statutes, emergency procedures, and legal remedies in plain language.

Explore Her Legal Guide

IT Rules 2021 Mandates for Platforms

Intermediary Guidelines requiring social media platforms to remove non-consensual explicit imagery/deepfakes within 24 hours of complaint and appoint India-based Grievance Officers.

Mandated 24h Takedown

Key Cyber Statutes (BNS 2023 & IT Act)

Covers Sections 66E (privacy violation), 67/67A (explicit media), BNS 77 (voyeurism), BNS 78 (cyber stalking), BNS 308 (extortion), POCSO Act 2012, and Indecent Representation of Women Act 1986.

Indian Penal Framework

3. Cyber Awareness & Digital Empowerment

Mass-reach awareness campaigns, digital skilling programmes, and community-level Cyber Sakhi initiatives.

CyberDost (I4C / Ministry of Home Affairs)

Official cyber safety handle by I4C/MHA on X, Instagram, and YouTube broadcasting real-time alerts on scam modus operandi (sextortion, digital arrests, deepfakes).

Follow @CyberDost

Digital Shakti Campaign (NCW + Meta)

Pan-India digital literacy and cyber safety skilling initiative by NCW, CyberPeace Foundation, and Meta, empowering over 600,000 women across India.

Digital Shakti Initiative

e-SafeHER / "Cyber Sakhi" Programme

MeitY & ISEA initiative with C-DAC Hyderabad to train 1 million rural women as community-level "Cyber Sakhis" through Self-Help Groups, building last-mile cyber confidence.

e-SafeHER Details

Cyber Jaagrookta Diwas

Nationwide monthly awareness day (held first Wednesday of every month) conducted by MHA across schools, colleges, Panchayats, and State police departments.

Monthly Awareness Day

4. Practical Cyber Hygiene & Safety Handbooks

Downloadable handbooks, password practices, and privacy setting guides tailored for women.

ISEA — Stay Safe Online (MeitY)

MeitY's core cyber hygiene portal publishing multilingual handbooks, posters, and videos including "Information Security Awareness Handbook for Women".

Visit staysafeonline.in

CERT-In Handbook for Mahila Suraksha

Official handbook by the Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In) containing step-by-step digital hygiene tips and device security rules for women.

Visit CERT-In Portal

Emerging Threats Against Women Guide

Specific ISEA resource cataloguing newer cyber threat vectors against women (deepfakes, sextortion, doxxing, stalkerware) with practical prevention advice.

Emerging Threats Page

Digital Evidence Preservation Protocol

Step-by-step guide to taking valid digital evidence: full uncropped screenshots with URL bar, system clock, message headers, and exporting chat logs prior to blocking offenders.

Evidence Checklist

5. Automated Take-Down & Platform Safety Tools

Privacy-first tools to hash and block non-consensual imagery across global social media networks.

StopNCII.org Hash Platform (Adults 18+)

Global platform operated by SWGfL generating unique digital hashes directly on your device (without uploading actual media) to prevent NCII distribution across major platforms.

Access StopNCII.org

Take It Down (For Minors Under 18)

NCMEC tool helping individuals under 18 (or parents/guardians) create device-side hashes to prevent explicit images/videos taken when under 18 from online platforms.

Visit Take It Down

Google Search Content Removal

Official Google webform to request the removal of non-consensual explicit personal imagery or personal information (doxxing) from Google Search results.

Google Removal Form

Safecity (Red Dot Foundation)

Crowdsourced anonymous reporting map of harassment incidents, cataloguing online harassment and public space abuse patterns to drive community safety advocacy.

Visit Safecity.in

6. Institutional Framework & International Toolkits

Enforcement portals and international tech safety guides for survivors and advocates.

SAHYOG & NCFL Laboratories

I4C/MHA law enforcement portals and National Cyber Forensic Laboratory (NCFL Dwarka) providing forensic analysis and issuing Section 79 takedown orders to ISPs.

MHA Law Enforcement

NNEDV TechSafety.org (Global Toolkit)

Comprehensive tech-abuse safety planning toolkit covering stalkerware detection, tech-facilitated domestic abuse, and survivor privacy strategies.

Visit TechSafety.org

Australia eSafety Commissioner Guides

Global benchmark model for image-based abuse education, offering survivor safety guides and educational toolkits on digital harassment response.

Visit eSafety.gov.au

CyberPeace Foundation

Indian civil society organization partnering across government initiatives (Digital Shakti, Cyber Rakshak) to deliver digital safety skilling and advocacy.

CyberPeace Foundation

An initiative dedicated to the protection, support, and empowerment of women through accurate and accessible information and resources.
